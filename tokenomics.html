PHELIX · Token schedule

PHELIX tokenomics: the full 4-year unlock schedule.

Every allocation, cliff and monthly release from the Token Generation Event (TGE) to month 48. Total supply is fixed at 1,000,000,000 PHELIX on BNB Chain at an initial fully diluted valuation of $50M ($0.05 per token). OpenHelix raised $5M in its strategic round at $0.025. Only 12% circulates at TGE, a $6M initial market cap; nothing can be minted later.

01 / Allocation

Where the 1 billion PHELIX go.

Seven allocations. Ecosystem, community and treasury hold 51% of supply and release slowly; strategic investors hold 20%; the team gets nothing for the first year.

1BPHELIX
02 / Valuation

Valuation and the $5M strategic round.

The listing price is set by the initial fully diluted valuation (FDV): $50M ÷ 1B = $0.05. Strategic investors bought 200M PHELIX at $0.025 — half the listing price — and their tokens vest over 24 months. Market cap counts only circulating tokens, so it starts far below the FDV.

Dollar figures use the initial listing price of $0.05 for planning. Once PHELIX trades, the market sets the price and these values will differ.
03 / Vesting terms

Cliffs and release schedule by allocation.

Each allocation unlocks a set share at TGE (month 0). The rest stays locked through the cliff, then releases in equal monthly amounts until the last unlock month.
