FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is OpenHelix?
A no-code token launcher. You create an ERC-20 token from your own wallet, set up liquidity on a trusted exchange, and manage the token from one page.
Which blockchains are supported?
BNB Chain, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, Optimism, Polygon and Avalanche mainnets, plus BNB Testnet, Sepolia and Base Sepolia for free practice. Solana is in progress.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. You fill in a short form (name, symbol, supply) and confirm in your wallet. The app builds and sends the transaction for you.
Does OpenHelix hold my funds or private keys?
Never. Every action is signed in your own wallet, such as MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet or Coinbase Wallet. OpenHelix has no access to your keys and no admin rights over your contract.
What does it cost?
OpenHelix charges nothing. You pay the blockchain's network fee, which the app shows before you confirm. Deploying uses about 800,000 gas. That is usually cheap on BNB Chain, Base and Polygon, and can be much more on Ethereum mainnet.
Is it safe to use on mainnet?
The token contract is built on OpenZeppelin's audited ERC-20 code. An independent audit of the OpenHelix contract itself is in progress, so run your first launch on a free testnet. Liquidity pools and LP locks are created on established third-party platforms, not held by OpenHelix.
Should I enable minting?
Only if you need it. Buyers and token scanners treat mintable tokens as riskier. If you enable it, you can renounce ownership later from My tokens, which disables minting forever.
Is this financial or legal advice?
No. OpenHelix is software tooling. Launching tokens and raising funds can carry legal and tax obligations that vary by country. Talk to a qualified lawyer and accountant before involving other people's money.
