OpenHelix Docs

Everything you need to create, manage and launch tokens with OpenHelix Studio — no code required.

Introduction

OpenHelix is a browser-based Web3 toolkit. From the Studio you can create ERC-20 tokens, manage them (transfer, burn, mint, renounce ownership), set up liquidity on a DEX, and download everything you need to verify the contract on the explorer — across seven EVM mainnets plus free testnets. You sign every action in your own wallet; OpenHelix never holds your keys or funds.

Getting started

You need three things:

  • An EVM browser wallet — MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet or Coinbase Wallet.
  • A little of the network's native coin for gas (free on testnets via a faucet).
  • The Studio open in your browser. Open the Studio →
Always practice on a testnet first. In the Studio, click Show testnets and pick BNB Testnet, Sepolia or Base Sepolia. Testnets behave like the real chains but use free, valueless coins.

Connecting a wallet

  1. Click Connect wallet in the top-right of the Studio.
  2. If you have more than one wallet installed, pick the one you want.
  3. Approve the connection. The button shows your shortened address and the bar shows the network your wallet is on.

Click your address at any time to see your balance, open it on the explorer, or disconnect. On a phone, open the site inside your wallet app's built-in browser.

Creating a token

Open the Create token tab, pick a network, fill in the form, and deploy:

  1. Name — the full display name (up to 40 characters).
  2. Symbol — the ticker, 2–11 letters or numbers.
  3. Total supply — whole tokens, minted in full to your wallet at creation.
  4. Decimals — 18 is standard.
  5. Permissions — optionally allow the owner to mint more later, and/or let holders burn their own tokens. These are fixed forever at deploy.

Click Deploy, review the summary, and confirm in your wallet. The Studio shows the estimated network fee first. OpenHelix charges no fee of its own. On success you get the contract address, an explorer link, and an Add to wallet button.

On mainnet, creating a token spends real gas and cannot be undone. Do a free testnet run first to confirm the flow in your wallet.

Managing a token

The My tokens tab lists tokens you created in this browser. Click one to see live supply, your balance and the owner, and to:

  • Transfer tokens to another address.
  • Burn tokens from your balance (if burning was enabled).
  • Mint new tokens and Renounce ownership (if minting was enabled). Renouncing disables minting forever and is a strong trust signal.

Created a token on another device? Use Import token and paste its address while your wallet is on the same network.

Adding liquidity

The Liquidity tab walks you through it: use the calculator to see the starting price your deposit creates, open PancakeSwap or Uniswap with your token pre-selected, then lock the LP tokens with an established locker (UNCX Network, Team Finance or PinkLock) for at least 90 days.

Built-in LP locking and vesting are in development and will reach mainnet only after an independent security audit.

Verifying source

Verification publishes your token's source code so anyone can read exactly what it does. It happens automatically right after you deploy — the success window shows when it's done. If it didn't finish, open the token in My tokens and click Verify automatically.

To verify by hand instead (always works, free on every explorer): click Download verification file, then on the explorer:

  1. Choose Verify and Publish → Solidity (Standard-JSON-Input).
  2. Compiler v0.8.24+commit.e11b9ed9, license MIT.
  3. Upload the file. If asked for the contract name, use OpenHelixToken.sol:OpenHelixToken.
  4. Constructor arguments are usually detected automatically; if not, click Copy constructor args on the token page and paste them.
Every OpenHelix token uses the same contract, so explorers often match new tokens automatically once one on that chain is verified.

Security & safety

  • Your keys stay yours. Every action is signed in your wallet. OpenHelix never sees your seed phrase or private key — and you should never paste it anywhere.
  • Use a dev/burner wallet while testing.
  • Testnet first, always. Confirm the whole flow for free before touching mainnet.
  • Audits before mainnet money. Fund-holding contracts (built-in locks, vesting, sales) will only go live after a professional audit.
  • Not legal or financial advice. Launching tokens or raising funds can carry legal and tax duties that vary by country — consult professionals.

Troubleshooting

"No wallet found"

Install MetaMask, Rabby, Trust Wallet or Coinbase Wallet, then reload the page. Wallets can't connect to pages shown inside preview frames — use the hosted site.

"Wrong network"

The Studio asks your wallet to switch (and adds the network if it's missing). Approve the prompt, or switch manually in your wallet.

Transaction fails or "insufficient funds"

You need the network's native coin for gas. On testnets, get free coins from that network's faucet and try again.

Something else

The deploy window and the pop-up messages show the exact error from your wallet or the network — that is the fastest way to diagnose the issue. Still stuck? Ask on Telegram.
